# Behavioural / Interaction Profile

**Owner:** BRIAN  
**Maturity:** TESTED analytical method  
**Current canonical implementation:** optional behavioural and interaction extension in [`BASIL Meeting Intelligence v2.0 FINAL`](../meeting-intelligence/SKILL.md)

The current BASIL implementation is not a separate standalone specialist skill. When the user requests a behavioural/interaction profile, BASIL routes that request through the Meeting Intelligence v2.0 extension using the finalised evidence substrate and confidently attributed speech.

The extension may analyse, where supported, turn-taking and interruption patterns, question/answer structure, explanatory versus decision-closing behaviour, challenge/correction patterns, topic-to-decision movement, role and organisational language, stable cross-meeting linguistic markers, reliably comparable acoustic/temporal patterns, interactional influence versus formal decision authority and longitudinal change.

Direct evidence outranks framework expectation. Ambiguous or mixed speech is unavailable participant-level evidence, not a licence to guess. Speaking time is descriptive and must not be treated as dominance, authority or influence without separate evidence. Do not diagnose personality or mental state.

Historical Meeting 01 and Meeting 02 profile outputs establish empirical use of the capability. They remain evidence and lineage, not authority to revive older specialist modules. Do not claim a separate historical framework/skill ran unless it actually existed and was invoked.
