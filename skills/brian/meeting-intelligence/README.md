Meeting Intelligence consumes finalised MANUEL evidence. It must not perform hidden transcription or forced speaker inference when the evidence layer has not established those facts.

Verified output contract includes:

- executive synthesis
- chronology and substantive themes
- participant positions and contributions
- decisions and agreements/disagreements
- commitments, requests and promises
- open questions
- risks and opportunities
- notable exact wording where supported
- what materially changed during the meeting
- follow-up separated into already committed, necessary administration and analyst recommendations
- transcript/speaker uncertainties
- compact action register with source lineage

It separates transcript-derived items from analyst inference and does not invent Importance, Urgency, deadlines or ownership.

The v1.9 run-close contract uses six headings: RUN STATUS, WHAT WAS PROCESSED, KEY RESULTS, EXCEPTIONS / UNRESOLVED ITEMS, WHERE THIS SITS IN BASIL and IMMEDIATE NEXT STEP. If finalisation gate G3 has not passed, the output must remain explicitly provisional/incomplete.
