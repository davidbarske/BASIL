# Purpose-driven Handoff — BASIL lab adaptation

A handoff exists so another session can continue, not to narrate everything that happened.

1. Identify the next session's purpose.
2. State current authority and live state needed for that purpose.
3. Reference existing artefacts by path/URL instead of copying their full contents.
4. Include decisions, unresolved questions, blockers and the immediate continuation point.
5. Include capability/status distinctions that matter to the next step.
6. Exclude secrets and personal evidence from public or portable handoffs unless the destination is authorised.
7. Verify referenced durable artefacts actually exist before relying on them.

BASIL handoffs remain subject to the stronger repository-persistence and portability rules of the wider project.
