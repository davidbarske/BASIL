# To Questionnaire — BASIL lab adaptation

Use when the operator cannot answer the substantive questions because the missing knowledge belongs to someone else.

Ask the operator only what they reliably know:

1. Who has the missing information? Capture their role, expertise and relationship to the problem.
2. What must BASIL/the operator be able to know, decide or do after the response?

Then generate a concise discovery questionnaire aimed directly at the gap between those two states.

Order questions by decision value. Keep each question to one idea. Explain why a question matters only where misunderstanding would reduce answer quality. Explicitly permit partial answers and uncertainty rather than encouraging confident invention.
